# Privacy Policy

This bot may have access to anything Discord allows it to see. That includes member profiles, usernames, roles, join and leave activity, and message content in channels it can read. Whether or not the bot uses all of this, assume it can see it.

Nothing is stored. There is no database, and nothing is written to disk or kept after the moment it happens. Once an event passes, it's gone. Bye bye :3

There is no outside network activity of any kind, except talking to Discord itself. No analytics, no third-party APIs, no external services. The bot doesn't send your data anywhere, because it has nowhere to send it ;w;

If you have questions or requests about your data, email lazzy.cipher@proton.me.
